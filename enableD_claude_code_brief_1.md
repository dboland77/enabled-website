# Claude Code Brief — enableD Website Rewrite
## getenabled.co.uk

---

## Context

enableD is a UK B2B SaaS product that helps employers manage reasonable adjustments for disabled employees. It is a real, live product at getenabled.co.uk. The app lives at app.getenabled.co.uk.

The founder is autistic, has fought through the employment tribunal system over a failed reasonable adjustments process, and has an appeal at the Employment Appeal Tribunal in November 2026. He built the product because he lived the problem firsthand.

The website currently has almost no content and is not converting. The entire job of the new website is a single thing: get an HR lead to send an email or book a call.

---

## The One Goal

Every element of the website serves one purpose: get an HR lead at a mid-sized UK employer to click "Get in Touch" and start a conversation.

The CTA should open an email to hello@getenabled.co.uk or link to a Calendly booking page (use a placeholder URL: https://calendly.com/getenabled for now).

There is no self-serve signup. No free trial CTA. Just: get in touch.

---

## Target Customer

**Primary:** HR Directors, Heads of People, HR Managers at UK employers with 100–500 employees.

**What keeps them up at night:** Legal exposure from getting reasonable adjustments wrong. Disjointed processes they can't evidence if challenged. The operational chaos of managing adjustments across email, spreadsheets, and Word documents.

**What they want:** A defensible, auditable process that protects the organisation and genuinely helps their disabled employees.

---

## Brand

- **Name:** enableD (the capital D is intentional — it signals disability without being heavy-handed)
- **Logo:** Available at https://www.getenabled.co.uk/logo/logo_full.svg
- **Existing nav items to preserve:** Features, About, Pricing, Blog, FAQ, Open App (links to app.getenabled.co.uk)
- **Tone:** Direct, honest, human. No corporate HR speak. No DEI buzzwords. No exclamation marks. Speaks to risk and consequence as much as values.
- **Existing colour palette:** Teal/dark navy from the logo — extend this into a refined, professional palette. Not purple gradients. Not generic SaaS blue.

## Design Direction

- Refined and serious. This is a product that deals with legal risk and disability rights — it should feel trustworthy and considered, not startup-bubbly.
- The teal/dark navy palette from the existing logo should anchor the design. Extend it with strong contrast and generous white space.
- Typography should be distinctive and confident. Not Inter or Roboto. Something with character that still reads as professional.
- The founder section should feel human and personal — consider a slightly different background treatment to distinguish it from the rest of the page.
- Mobile responsive throughout.
- Subtle animations on scroll are fine. Nothing that feels like a tech demo.
- No stock photography of smiling office workers. No generic diversity imagery.

---

## Technical Notes

- Preserve existing navigation structure and links
- CTA buttons: primary action is mailto:hello@getenabled.co.uk OR link to https://calendly.com/getenabled (placeholder)
- "Open App" link: https://app.getenabled.co.uk
- Logo: https://www.getenabled.co.uk/logo/logo_full.svg
- The site appears to be a static or simple frontend — match whatever stack is already in use if possible, otherwise clean HTML/CSS/JS is fine
- Do not add self-serve signup flows, pricing tables, or feature grids to the homepage — those exist on other pages already
